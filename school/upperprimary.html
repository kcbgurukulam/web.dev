Deepening academic rigor, analytical inquiry, ethical leadership, and scientific reasoning across Classes VI and VII.

Classes VI & VII • Expanding to Class 10 Next Year!

Upper Primary is where students cultivate independent reasoning, analytical depth, and mature study ethics. Following the NEP 2020 aligned CBSE syllabus, our curriculum is presently active up to Class VII, and expanding up to Class 10 (Class X) starting next academic year. This gives parents complete peace of mind with continuous, uninterrupted schooling right through high school and board exam excellence.
