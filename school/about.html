Welcome to KCB Gurukulam

KCB Gurukulam is an institution dedicated to holistic knowledge and steadfast character building, located beside KCB Gardens on New Housing Board Road, Adilabad, Telangana.

Founded by K C Bhargava, the school delivers a contemporary, CBSE-aligned experiential curriculum. Here, noble human values, life skills, and the venerable Guru-shishya tradition meet modern pedagogy and technology-driven teaching.

Message from the Founder

K C Bhargava

Founder & Correspondent

"When I envisioned KCB Gurukulam, my goal was to redefine education in our community. We believe a student's geography should never limit their potential, which is why we are dedicated to uplifting Adilabad through world-class, technology-driven, value-grounded learning."

"At KCB Gurukulam, value education and life skills development are coordinated with character education to sculpt every child into a complete student today and a strong leader tomorrow."

Under the direct guidance of K C Bhargava, KCB Gurukulam implements child-centric methodologies, continuous teacher training, and an open door policy for parents. We invite every parent to be a partner in this transformative educational journey.
