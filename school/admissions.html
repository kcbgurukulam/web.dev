Admissions Help & FAQs

Common questions parents ask about applying to KCB Gurukulam English Medium School, Adilabad.

Which classes are eligible for admission in 2026-27? expand_more

Admissions are open for Pre-Primary (Nursery, LKG, UKG), Primary (Classes I to V), and Upper Primary (Classes VI and VII). Please note that our school offerings are strictly capped at Class 7, ensuring dedicated attention to formative learning years.

What documents are required for completing the admission? expand_more

Parents should provide: (1) Copy of Child's Birth Certificate, (2) Previous school Transfer Certificate (TC) & Progress Report (for Class 1 upwards), (3) Child & Parent Aadhaar card copies, and (4) 4 recent passport-size photographs of the student.

Does KCB Gurukulam offer school bus transport throughout Adilabad? expand_more

Yes. We operate safe school bus routes covering all prominent neighborhoods in Adilabad town. Each bus is speed-governed at 40 km/h, equipped with GPS tracking, and staffed with an attentive attendant for maximum child safety.

Can I visit the school campus before applying? expand_more

Absolutely! Parents are warmly invited to visit our campus beside KCB Gardens, New Housing Board Road, Adilabad, between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM (Monday to Saturday) to tour the classrooms, science labs, computer rooms, and speak directly with our academic coordinators.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio maintained in classes? expand_more

We maintain an optimal 1:18 teacher-student ratio across all wings, ensuring personalized mentorship, regular individual evaluation, and tailored support for every child's academic and emotional growth.
