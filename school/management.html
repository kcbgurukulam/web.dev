Management & Leadership

Visionary leadership dedicated to academic excellence, ethical stewardship, and community empowerment in Adilabad.

lightbulb Founder's Vision

K C Bhargava, a seasoned software professional and former NRI, established KCB Gurukulam with a singular calling: to bring world-class education rooted in timeless Indian values to the children of Adilabad.

Driven by the belief that geographical location should never limit a child's aspirations, he leads the institution to blend CBSE academic rigor, digital fluency, and moral character into a holistic learning journey.

"A true school is not just a place of syllabus; it is a sacred space for sculpting resilient character, noble human values, and fearless curiosity. At KCB Gurukulam, we are shaping the future leaders and thinkers of tomorrow."

— K C Bhargava, Founder & Correspondent

K C Bhargava

Founder & Correspondent

corporate_fare Operational Stewardship

K. Padmavathi Bhargava oversees the day-to-day administrative operations, infrastructure development, student pastoral care, and long-term campus expansion at KCB Gurukulam.

Ensures campus security, school bus operations & transit safety, health hygiene standards, and parental communication flow smoothly every single day.

"Our commitment at KCB Gurukulam is to provide a home away from home where every child feels cherished, inspired, and motivated to achieve their highest personal and academic potential."

— K. Padmavathi Bhargava, Managing Director

K. Padmavathi Bhargava

psychology Academic Leadership

T. Nivedeta leads the academic life and pedagogical standards of KCB Gurukulam. With rich instructional experience, she guides curriculum delivery, experiential lesson planning, and student progress monitoring.

Responsible for teacher training, student discipline, interactive smart-classroom learning, and cultivating a joyful, inquiry-driven environment across foundational and preparatory wings.

"Education is about igniting curious minds and fostering ethical leadership. Every day at KCB Gurukulam is an opportunity for discovery and purposeful growth."
