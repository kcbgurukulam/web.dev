1. Institutional Governance & Framework

Foundational Ethics & CBSE Framework

KCB Gurukulam is an English Medium Educational Institution located beside KCB Gardens, New Housing Board Road, Adilabad, Telangana. Founded by K C Bhargava, the institution operates under the CBSE curriculum framework, blending traditional Guru-shishya values with contemporary academic rigor. By accessing this website or enrolling a student, parents and guardians agree to abide by these institutional Terms and Regulations.

how_to_reg

2. Admission Policy & Document Verification

Pre-Primary to Class VII

Admissions for the 2026-27 academic session are active from Pre-Primary (Nursery, LKG, UKG) to Class VII:

  • Age Guidelines: Admissions follow government and board age criteria. Proof of age (Municipal/Gram Panchayat Birth Certificate) is mandatory.
  • Document Authenticity: Parents must furnish authentic Transfer Certificates (TC), student and parental Aadhaar cards, and previous academic progress reports. Submission of fraudulent records is grounds for immediate admission forfeiture.
  • Merit & Availability: Classroom seats are subject to availability to uphold our strict 1:18 student-teacher ratio.
payments

3. Transparent Fee Regulations

No Capitation • Transparent Structure

KCB Gurukulam strictly opposes capitation fees or hidden levies. Fee structures are clearly communicated at the admissions desk:

  • Fees must be remitted as per the published schedule (annual or term installments) to ensure uninterrupted educational services.
  • Fees once paid towards admission processing or completed terms are non-refundable, except as expressly provided under state regulatory norms.
  • Transport fees are calculated based on designated route distances and must be settled alongside school term dues.
self_improvement

4. Code of Conduct & Discipline

Gurukul Values • Mutual Respect

To maintain a safe, uplifting learning environment, all students and guardians are expected to observe high ethical standards:

  • Punctuality & Attendance: Regular attendance is mandatory. Parents must submit written leave notes for unforeseen absences.
  • Anti-Bullying Zero Tolerance: Strict zero-tolerance policies exist against bullying, aggressive conduct, harassment, or verbal abuse on campus or on school transit.
  • Campus Uniform: Students must attend school in clean, prescribed uniform with photo identification badges.
directions_bus

5. School Transport & Commute Guidelines

Speed-Governed Safety Protocols

School transit is an optional support facility operated under stringent safety standards:

  • Buses operate under strict speed governors (maximum 40 km/h) and designated stops across Adilabad town.
  • Parents must ensure children are escorted to and received from designated stops punctually.
  • Transit routes are planned by school administration; individual door-to-door deviations cannot be guaranteed.
